# Endings and asks

Where viewers leave at the end, and where an ask costs the least. Limits by length are in [by-length.md](by-length.md).

## The rules

**E1. End right after the payoff** (high as advice, medium in practice; engine: `RETENTION tail`; skill for the lines).

- Shorts: 3 s or less after the payoff. Hoyos trimmed one second from a Short's end and retention went from 83% to 88%.
- Long videos: about 20 s or less of content after the payoff, then 5 to 20 s of end-screen room pointing to the next video. Put that room in the outro card, which `tail` doesn't count.
- Every source says no recap, no "in summary", no early "that's it for today": MrBeast's guide, Wistia's data (a summary cost about 40 s of viewing), Blackman and TEDx. Yet 3 or 4 of the 6 hits at 3, 5 and 20 min recap or signal the end, 73 to 96% of the way through, and they did fine. So flag a recap line and offer to cut it, but don't insist.

Lines to flag in the last stretch: "so to recap", "in summary", "that's about it", "that's all for today", "before you go", "thanks for watching" before the payoff has landed.

**E2. No ask before value** (medium, skill flag; the wording is a script job). No like or subscribe ask before the first payoff. A generic "like and subscribe" had no measurable effect on subscribes or comments across 8,500 videos (JRIM). A spoken ask beats end text, and a specific ask beats a generic one: "watch this one next", a link, a question that invites a real comment.

**E3. Loop endings on Shorts** (medium, skill). When the last line leads back into the first, cut so the end flows into the start. Replays count as views but not as engaged views, so a loop helps reach more than revenue. Flag a candidate seam; don't build one by cutting mid-thought.

**E4. Clips stand alone** (high, existing: `video-clips`). No opening pronoun that needs missing setup ("so this is why it..."), a hook title, and an end on the payoff.

## Asks by length

| | Where the ask goes |
|---|---|
| 60 s | none, or one spoken line over the payoff |
| 3 min | at the end, in the last quarter |
| 5 min | at the end, plus tool links where each tool is used |
| 20 min | an early ask after the first payoff is fine; a sponsor at 20 to 57%, on a chapter change, with the screen moving |
| 60 min | at most 2 breaks; links in the description |

No hit video asked for a subscribe mid-video.

## In the plan

- Find the payoff: the last line that delivers what the title promised. Everything after it is tail.
- `director cut` the recap or wrap-up lines after it, keeping the closing beat (about half a second) after the payoff's last word.
- No fade to black and no end card over 1.4 s unless Joe asks (already in the edit skill). For YouTube end-screen room on a long video, the outro card or a held final shot of 5 to 20 s is the exception, and only when Joe will add an end screen.
- A lower-third or title for a link goes on the word that names it.

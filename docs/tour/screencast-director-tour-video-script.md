# Screencast Director: the tour

**Format:** In-app tour (plays from Getting Started) and the README demo, about 2 min
**Words:** about 235 (under 2 min at 140 words a minute, plus room for clicking)
**Audience:** someone who just opened Screencast Director, or is deciding whether to
**Look:** the Personal Dark profile. No other profiles in the Profile menu while recording (see Before you record).
**Angle:** You talk through your demo once, and the app does the boring part of the edit.

---

## Before you record

- Settings > General: **Profile** set to Personal Dark. Settings > Recording: **Show this app in recordings** on.
- Move any work profiles out of `~/Library/Application Support/Screencast Director/Profiles/` for the take, so the Profile menu only lists Personal and Personal Dark. Put them back after.
- The library should hold only recordings you're happy to show. `publish-demo` (already in Personal Dark) is the sample recording; open it once first so its preview is loaded.
- Start the take from the menu bar or ⌘N. Don't press ⌃⌥⌘R during the take: it stops it.

---

*[Camera: Joe, full frame.]*

I recorded this video in this app, and I barely edited it. The app did most of it.

So this is Screencast Director. Not director like Spielberg. Then again, Spielberg yells "cut" a lot, and so does this app.

*[Screen: the start page. Point at New Recording.]*

Press New Recording, or Control, Option, Command, R from any app. It records your screen, your mic, and your camera.

*[Screen: open publish-demo. Its draft edit is already there.]*

When you stop, it transcribes you on your Mac and drafts the edit with one rule. I call it the talking rule. Talking plays in real time. Typing without talking is fast-forward. And if nothing's happening, it's gone.

*[Skit: Joe: "Hey, so I spent like eleven seconds looking for my cursor." App voice, flat: "Gone."]*

It also zooms in where you click and captions what you said.

*[Screen: the timeline. Click a dashed cut, then Restore. Press Z and drag the zoom box.]*

Blue plays in real time, orange is sped up, dashed is cut. Click a cut to put it back. Z adds a zoom; drag the box to aim it.

*[Screen: the Ask box. Type "cut my retakes and zoom in when I mention the timeline". Let the steps run.]*

Or just ask. "Cut my retakes and zoom in when I mention the timeline." It reads the transcript, edits the timeline, and shows you what it changed. Command Z undoes it.

*[Screen: the inspector. Set Shape to 9:16, then open Export.]*

Set the shape to 9:16 and it crops to follow your clicks, so it reads on a phone. Export has presets for YouTube and Shorts, and Publish writes your captions, chapters, and a description. It even picks a thumbnail where you're smiling.

*[Camera: Joe, a big forced grin.]*

So smile once. Like this. *[Beat.]* That's a thumbnail now.

*[Camera: Joe, full frame.]*

You talk, and the app handles the boring part. That's the talking rule.

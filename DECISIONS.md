# Edit decisions

Joe's standing verdicts for how his videos look and sound, one per line, dated. Read before changing a profile, effect, plan default or render; add each new verdict the moment Joe gives it. A line here outranks a mockup, a skill default or a profile.

## Look
- 2026-11-04: No blue corner bars or corner shapes, in any profile ("the second time I told you I hate them").
- 2026-11-04: Color grading stays minimal: "4k beautiful", only slightly tuned. Not too green, not too warm.
- 2026-11-04: Scan lines, if used at all, are way more minimal.
- 2026-11-05: The personal profile follows joekarlsson.com: scribbly, hot pink. 2026-11-11: squiggles are pink crayons that boil and draw in, like on the site.
- 2026-11-06: Title cards are small and low. They never cover Joe's face.
- 2026-11-04: The Vox-style look should feel alive (shimmer, movement), never dead and static.

## Framing
- 2026-11-02: While Joe demos an app, show the screen, not his face.
- 2026-11-02: Zoom goes to where the cursor is now, not where it is headed.
- 2026-11-02: The cutout never cuts off Joe's shoulders.
- 2026-11-04: Vertical videos keep text and faces inside the TikTok and Instagram safe zones. Nothing sits under the top bars.

## Sound and memes
- 2026-11-04: Fewer pop-up sounds: not on every overlay or switch. Vary the sketching and glitch effects so they don't get samey (2026-11-02, 2026-11-04).
- 2026-11-04: Use real sound effects YouTubers use, popular meme sounds included. Don't stall on licensing for sounds everyone uses.
- 2026-11-10: Memes sit slightly rotated, pop in with a pop sound, then shrink away.
